MAP-Elites with the primary (PPO-track) detector as validity -- checks (cloud, 2 CPU cores)
==========================================================================================
Changed: MAP_Elites/problem.py (is_valid, VALIDITY_DETECTORS), MAP_Elites/map_elites.py
(--validity, stored in config.json), MAP_Elites/robustness.py (--detector).
New: MAP_Elites/descriptor_sampling.py (re-judges the 401 genomes of the original LHS study).
Test outputs were deleted after the checks.

1. Reproduction of the original study (validity section04, first 21 genomes = default + 20 LHS)
   - 20/21 verdicts equal the original samples.json; descriptors identical (max |d duration| = 0).
   - The one difference (LHS #16: original FAIL "exceeded one-flip rotation/travel budget",
     travel 520.8 deg; now valid) is the documented 2026-09-29 detector change (travel counted
     only until the vehicle is upright again): "some old failures may now pass".

2. Primary validity (first 61 genomes)
   - ppo_track:        46/60 LHS valid (77 %); failures: 13 no 0.4 s hold, 1 crash/region.
                       2 of the 46 valid samples end with a revolution of ~720 deg (double flip):
                       the primary detector does not limit the number of turns.
   - ppo_track + single-turn check: 44/60 valid; the 2 double flips are rejected ("more than one turn").
     (Tested under the earlier option name ppo_track_1turn; renamed to the flag --single-turn-check,
     re-checked with constructed results: a latched 720 deg success is valid without the check and
     invalid with it, 361 deg stays valid, a crash after the latch is invalid; the flag is refused
     without --validity ppo_track and on --resume.)
   - Every section04-valid genome is also ppo_track-valid (first 21 genomes).

3. map_elites.py (--validity ppo_track, budget 30 then --resume to 40, 2 workers)
   - config.json stores "validity": "ppo_track"; resume keeps it (40 evaluations, 21 cells).
   - --resume together with --validity is refused.
   - A config.json without the field (ME1-ME3, seed replicates) loads as section04.
   - An unknown validity is rejected.

4. robustness.py (--detector ppo_track, --top 1 --episodes 1 --confirm 0)
   - config.json stores "detector": "ppo_track"; a section04 screening writes no such key,
     so the config of R1 is unchanged and R1 can still be resumed.
   - success and failure_mode columns follow the primary detector (report.succeeded / failure_mode).
