# Reward iteration log (§03)

Kept live during training, as the methodology requires. Every run uses the same environment (course fork, untouched), action-repeat 4 (50 Hz decisions) and PPO setup unless stated. Progress is judged by the **independent §04 detector** on deterministic evaluation episodes (10 s, seeds 10000+), never by the training return.

The price: under gyro noise the PID does not settle (20% vs 100% for pure PPO) |
6. **Give each block the job its structure is good at.** A memoryless policy learned the agile, noise-robust part (the flip). Steady-state disturbance rejection needs integral action, which the PID has by construction and the policy could not learn in 300k steps. The hybrid gets the best position hold, and pure PPO gets the best noise robustness. The report should present that trade-off, not hide it.
